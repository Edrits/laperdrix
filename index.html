The place

A monastery with a swimming pool and too many walnut trees.

Thirteenth century, gated, on the edge of the village. Three houses joined by a passage, a vine terrace that takes the afternoon shade, and a church steeple to watch the sun go behind. Johnny and Ingrid have lived here since 2007 and it is, by their own account, a little ramshackle.

Nothing to organise up here. Put your dates in further down when you know them.

Twenty minutes away

Brantôme, where the abbey is cut straight into the cliff.
